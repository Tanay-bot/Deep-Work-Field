Part III — Answer key (teacher copy)

4b. Limiting reagent

Al: 10.0 g ÷ 26.98 = 0.3706 mol. Cl₂: 10.0 g ÷ 70.90 = 0.1410 mol.

Cl₂ needs 0.1410 × (2/3) = 0.0940 mol Al, which is less than 0.3706 mol, so Cl₂ is limiting.

AlCl₃: 0.1410 × (2/3) = 0.0940 mol × 133.34 = 12.5 g theoretical yield.
